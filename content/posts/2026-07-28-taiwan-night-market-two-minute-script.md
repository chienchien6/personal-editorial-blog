# 台灣夜市 2 分鐘英文導覽稿：逛夜市、吃小吃、玩遊戲

## 可能抽到的題目

- 介紹台灣夜市
- 介紹台灣夜生活
- 介紹適合外國旅客的美食景點

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce night markets in Taiwan. Night markets are one of the most popular places for visitors to experience local life.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台灣夜市。夜市是旅客體驗在地生活最受歡迎的地方之一。

At a night market, visitors can try many kinds of street food, such as oyster omelets, stinky tofu, fried chicken, scallion pancakes, bubble tea, and shaved ice.
:::zh 在夜市，旅客可以品嚐很多小吃，例如蚵仔煎、臭豆腐、炸雞、蔥油餅、珍珠奶茶和剉冰。

Besides food, night markets also have small games, clothes, souvenirs, and daily products. The atmosphere is lively, friendly, and full of energy.
:::zh 除了食物，夜市也有小遊戲、衣服、伴手禮和生活用品。氣氛熱鬧、友善，而且充滿活力。

For first-time visitors, I would recommend going with friends and sharing different foods. This way, they can try more flavors without getting too full.
:::zh 對第一次來的旅客，我會建議和朋友一起去，大家分食不同食物。這樣可以嘗到更多味道，也不會太快吃飽。

I think night markets show the everyday creativity of Taiwan. They are not fancy, but they are warm, fun, and unforgettable.
:::zh 我認為夜市展現出台灣日常的創意。它們不華麗，但很溫暖、有趣，也令人難忘。

## 30 秒短版

Taiwanese night markets are lively places where visitors can eat, shop, and play games. Popular foods include oyster omelets, stinky tofu, fried chicken, bubble tea, and shaved ice. I recommend sharing food with friends so visitors can try many flavors. Night markets show the warm and energetic side of Taiwan.
:::zh 台灣夜市是很熱鬧的地方，旅客可以吃東西、購物和玩遊戲。受歡迎的食物包括蚵仔煎、臭豆腐、炸雞、珍珠奶茶和剉冰。我建議和朋友分食，這樣可以嘗到更多味道。夜市展現出台灣溫暖又有活力的一面。

## 單字

- night market：夜市
- lively atmosphere：熱鬧氣氛
- souvenir：伴手禮
- first-time visitor：第一次來的旅客
- share different foods：分食不同食物
