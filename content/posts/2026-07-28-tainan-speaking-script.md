# 台南英文導覽稿：古都、小吃、廟宇與歷史街區

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Tainan, one of the oldest cities in Taiwan.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台南，台灣最古老的城市之一。

Tainan is often called the old capital of Taiwan. It is famous for historical sites, temples, traditional streets, and local food.
:::zh 台南常被稱為台灣古都。它以歷史景點、廟宇、傳統街區和在地美食聞名。

For history, visitors can go to Anping Fort and Chihkan Tower. These places help visitors understand Taiwan's early history and cultural development.
:::zh 歷史方面，旅客可以去安平古堡和赤崁樓。這些地方能幫助旅客理解台灣早期歷史和文化發展。

For culture, Tainan has many temples. Temple visits are a good way to see traditional architecture, local beliefs, and community life.
:::zh 文化方面，台南有很多廟宇。參觀廟宇是欣賞傳統建築、地方信仰和社群生活的好方法。

For food, Tainan is a paradise. Visitors can try beef soup, shrimp rolls, coffin bread, danzai noodles, and many sweet local snacks.
:::zh 美食方面，台南像是天堂。旅客可以品嚐牛肉湯、蝦捲、棺材板、擔仔麵和許多甜味在地小吃。

I recommend Tainan to travelers who love history, culture, and food. It is a city where visitors can slow down and feel the traditional side of Taiwan.
:::zh 我推薦台南給喜歡歷史、文化和美食的旅客。這是一座能讓旅客放慢腳步，感受台灣傳統面貌的城市。

## 30 秒短版

Tainan is one of the oldest cities in Taiwan and is often called the old capital. It is famous for historical sites, temples, old streets, and local food. Visitors can go to Anping Fort, Chihkan Tower, and local temples, and try beef soup, shrimp rolls, and danzai noodles. Tainan is perfect for travelers who love culture and food.
:::zh 台南是台灣最古老的城市之一，常被稱為古都。它以歷史景點、廟宇、老街和在地美食聞名。旅客可以去安平古堡、赤崁樓和廟宇，也可以品嚐牛肉湯、蝦捲和擔仔麵。台南很適合喜歡文化和美食的旅客。

## 單字

- old capital：古都
- historical site：歷史景點
- traditional street：傳統街區
- local belief：地方信仰
- food paradise：美食天堂
