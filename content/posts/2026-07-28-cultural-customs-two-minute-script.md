# 台灣文化習俗 2 分鐘英文導覽稿：拜拜、紅包、廟口文化

## 可能抽到的題目

- 介紹台灣文化習俗
- 介紹台灣廟宇文化
- 介紹一個外國人會覺得有趣的習俗

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce three parts of Taiwanese culture: temple worship, red envelopes, and temple-front community life.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台灣文化的三個部分：拜拜、紅包和廟口生活。

First, temple worship is common in Taiwan. People visit temples to pray for health, safety, good luck, or success. Temples are also places where visitors can see traditional architecture and local festivals.
:::zh 首先，拜拜在台灣很常見。人們會到廟裡祈求健康、平安、好運或成功。廟宇也是旅客欣賞傳統建築和地方節慶的地方。

Second, red envelopes are important during Lunar New Year. Older family members give red envelopes to children or younger relatives as a blessing for the new year.
:::zh 第二，紅包在農曆新年很重要。長輩會給小孩或晚輩紅包，象徵新年的祝福。

Third, temple-front areas are often lively community spaces. People can find food stalls, small shops, local events, and everyday conversations there.
:::zh 第三，廟口常常是熱鬧的社群空間。人們可以在那裡找到小吃攤、小店、地方活動和日常聊天。

I think these customs show that Taiwanese culture is not only traditional, but also part of everyday life.
:::zh 我認為這些習俗展現出台灣文化不只是傳統，也存在日常生活裡。

## 30 秒短版

Taiwanese culture can be seen in temples, family traditions, and daily life. People visit temples to pray for good luck and safety. During Lunar New Year, older family members give red envelopes as blessings. Around temples, visitors can also find food stalls and local activities. These customs show the warm side of Taiwan.
:::zh 台灣文化可以從廟宇、家庭傳統和日常生活中看見。人們會到廟裡祈求好運和平安。農曆新年時，長輩會發紅包表示祝福。廟宇周邊也常有小吃攤和地方活動。這些習俗展現出台灣溫暖的一面。

## 單字

- temple worship：拜拜
- red envelope：紅包
- blessing：祝福
- food stall：小吃攤
- community space：社群空間
