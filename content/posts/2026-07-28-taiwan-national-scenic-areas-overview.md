# 國家風景區英文口說總覽：山、海、湖、縱谷與離島怎麼講

國家風景區題目很廣，但可以用一個簡單分類記住：山、海、湖、縱谷、離島。

回答時不要把景點名稱全部背完。比較穩的是選 2 到 3 個代表例子，說明台灣自然景觀很多元。

## 可能抽到的題目

- 介紹台灣國家風景區
- 介紹台灣自然景觀
- 介紹一個適合外國旅客的戶外行程
- 介紹山區或海岸旅遊
- 規劃一個自然景點行程

## 2 分鐘英文導覽稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Taiwan's national scenic areas. Although Taiwan is not a large island, it has very diverse landscapes, including mountains, coastlines, lakes, valleys, and offshore islands.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台灣的國家風景區。雖然台灣不是很大的島，但它有非常多元的景觀，包括山、海岸、湖泊、縱谷和離島。

For mountain scenery, Alishan is one of the most famous destinations. Visitors can enjoy sunrise views, forest trails, old trees, and the historic forest railway.
:::zh 如果是山區景觀，阿里山是最有名的目的地之一。旅客可以欣賞日出、森林步道、神木和歷史悠久的森林鐵路。

For lake scenery, Sun Moon Lake is a classic choice. Visitors can take a boat, ride a bike around the lake, and learn about the local Indigenous culture.
:::zh 如果是湖景，日月潭是很經典的選擇。旅客可以搭船、環湖騎車，也可以認識當地原住民文化。

For eastern Taiwan, the East Coast and the East Longitudinal Valley are very special. Travelers can enjoy ocean views, rice fields, mountains, cycling routes, and hot springs.
:::zh 如果是台灣東部，東海岸和花東縱谷很有特色。旅客可以欣賞海景、稻田、山景、自行車路線和溫泉。

These scenic areas are not only beautiful. They also show the relationship between nature, local culture, and everyday life in Taiwan.
:::zh 這些風景區不只是美麗，也展現出台灣自然、地方文化和日常生活之間的關係。

I would recommend visitors choose one scenic area and spend enough time there, instead of rushing through too many places.
:::zh 我會建議旅客選一個風景區，並留足夠時間慢慢體驗，而不是匆忙跑太多地方。

## 30 秒短版

Taiwan has many national scenic areas with diverse landscapes. Visitors can go to Alishan for sunrise and forest trails, Sun Moon Lake for lake views and cycling, and eastern Taiwan for ocean views, rice fields, and hot springs. These places show both the natural beauty and local culture of Taiwan.
:::zh 台灣有許多國家風景區，景觀非常多元。旅客可以去阿里山看日出和森林步道，去日月潭欣賞湖景和騎自行車，也可以去台灣東部看海景、稻田和泡溫泉。這些地方展現出台灣的自然之美和地方文化。

## 可替換句型

- This area is famous for its natural scenery.
- Visitors can enjoy both outdoor activities and local culture.
- It is suitable for travelers who love nature.
- I recommend staying longer instead of rushing.
- This destination shows a different side of Taiwan.

## 實用單字

- national scenic area：國家風景區
- diverse landscapes：多元景觀
- coastline：海岸線
- lake view：湖景
- valley：縱谷
- offshore island：離島
- forest trail：森林步道
- sunrise view：日出景觀
- Indigenous culture：原住民文化
- outdoor activity：戶外活動
