# 台灣水果王國英文口說腳本：用台東鳳梨、池上米回答農產品題

「介紹台灣水果王國」這類題目很適合用白話英文回答。

考官通常不是要聽艱深的農業專有名詞，而是想知道你能不能把台灣特色講得清楚、生動，而且像真的在推薦旅客。

這篇用台東旅遊經驗當素材，把水果、農產品、地方創意和東部景觀串成一段 2 到 3 分鐘的英文口說稿。

## 這篇可以對應的抽題

- 介紹台灣水果王國
- 介紹台灣農產品
- 介紹台灣東部特色
- 介紹一個適合外國旅客體驗在地文化的行程
- 介紹台灣美食或伴手禮

## 開場：點出台灣水果王國

Good morning or afternoon. Taiwan is widely known as the Fruit Kingdom. Because of our warm weather and rich soil, we have many delicious fruits and agricultural products all year round.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。台灣廣為人知的美譽是水果王國。因為我們有溫暖的氣候和肥沃的土壤，一年四季都有許多美味水果和農產品。

For visitors, tasting local fruits is not only about food. It is also a great way to experience Taiwan's local culture and everyday life.
:::zh 對旅客來說，品嚐在地水果不只是吃東西，也是體驗台灣地方文化和日常生活的好方法。

## 結合個人經驗：從台東切入

Since I recently took a trip to Taitung, I would like to introduce some special agricultural products from eastern Taiwan.
:::zh 因為我最近去台東旅行，所以我想介紹一些台灣東部很有特色的農產品。

Taitung is a relaxing place with beautiful scenery, open fields, and many local foods. It is a good destination for travelers who want to slow down and enjoy nature.
:::zh 台東是一個很放鬆的地方，有美麗風景、開闊田野和許多在地美食。它很適合想放慢腳步、親近自然的旅客。

## 例子一：鳳梨與在地創意

Taitung is famous for pineapples. They are sweet, juicy, and full of tropical flavor.
:::zh 台東以鳳梨聞名。台東鳳梨甜、多汁，而且充滿熱帶風味。

When I visited the Luye area for the Hot Air Balloon Festival, I tried a special drink called pineapple coffee at a local vintage grocery shop.
:::zh 當我去鹿野參加熱氣球嘉年華時，我在當地一間復古柑仔店喝到一種很特別的飲品，叫做鳳梨咖啡。

At first, pineapple and coffee may sound like a surprising combination, but it shows how creative Taiwanese people can be with local farm products.
:::zh 一開始，鳳梨和咖啡聽起來可能是很意外的組合，但它展現了台灣人如何有創意地運用在地農產品。

## 例子二：池上米與鄉村景觀

Besides fruits, Taiwan is also known for high-quality rice. One of the best examples is Chishang rice from Taitung.
:::zh 除了水果，台灣也以高品質稻米聞名，其中一個最好的例子就是台東池上米。

If you visit Chishang, you can ride a bike along Mr. Brown Avenue. You will be surrounded by wide green rice paddies and beautiful mountain views.
:::zh 如果你到池上，可以沿著伯朗大道騎腳踏車。你會被大片綠色稻田和美麗山景包圍。

This experience helps visitors understand why rice is so important in Taiwanese food culture.
:::zh 這樣的體驗能幫助旅客理解，為什麼米飯在台灣飲食文化中這麼重要。

## 結尾：邀請旅客體驗

In my opinion, the best way to understand Taiwan is to taste our local fruits and farm products in the places where they are grown.
:::zh 我認為，理解台灣最好的方式，就是在農產品生長的地方親自品嚐在地水果和農產品。

That is why I highly recommend visiting Taitung. You can enjoy pineapples, rice fields, local drinks, and beautiful scenery in one trip.
:::zh 這也是為什麼我很推薦去台東。你可以在同一趟旅行中體驗鳳梨、稻田、在地飲品和美麗風景。

Thank you.
:::zh 謝謝。

## 30 秒短版

Taiwan is known as the Fruit Kingdom because we have warm weather, rich soil, and many fruits all year round. I would like to use Taitung as an example. Taitung is famous for sweet pineapples, and I once tried pineapple coffee in Luye. Besides fruits, Chishang rice is also very famous. Visitors can ride bikes along Mr. Brown Avenue and enjoy beautiful rice paddies. I think tasting local fruits and farm products is one of the best ways to experience Taiwanese culture.
:::zh 台灣被稱為水果王國，因為我們有溫暖氣候、肥沃土壤和一年四季的水果。我想用台東當例子。台東以甜美鳳梨聞名，我曾經在鹿野喝過鳳梨咖啡。除了水果，池上米也很有名。旅客可以沿著伯朗大道騎腳踏車，欣賞美麗稻田。我認為品嚐在地水果和農產品，是體驗台灣文化最好的方式之一。

## 可替換句型

如果忘記水果名稱，可以這樣說：

- It is a local fruit from Taiwan.
- It tastes sweet and juicy.
- It is popular in summer.
- Many visitors like to buy it as a gift.
- You can try it at local markets.

如果想把主題拉回旅遊，可以這樣說：

- This is not only a food experience, but also a cultural experience.
- Visitors can learn about local life through food.
- I recommend trying it in the place where it is produced.

## 實用單字

- Fruit Kingdom：水果王國
- agricultural products：農產品
- local farm products：在地農產品
- warm weather：溫暖氣候
- rich soil：肥沃土壤
- pineapple：鳳梨
- juicy：多汁的
- tropical flavor：熱帶風味
- rice paddies：稻田
- local culture：在地文化
