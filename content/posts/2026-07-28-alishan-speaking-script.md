# 阿里山英文導覽稿：日出、森林鐵路、神木與高山茶

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Alishan, one of Taiwan's most famous mountain destinations.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹阿里山，台灣最有名的山區目的地之一。

Alishan is famous for sunrise views, a sea of clouds, forest trails, old trees, and the historic forest railway.
:::zh 阿里山以日出、雲海、森林步道、神木和歷史悠久的森林鐵路聞名。

Many visitors wake up early to watch the sunrise. When the sun rises over the mountains, the view is peaceful and unforgettable.
:::zh 許多旅客會早起看日出。當太陽從山間升起時，景色寧靜又難忘。

The Alishan Forest Railway is also very special. It takes visitors through forests and mountain scenery, and it shows an important part of Taiwan's history.
:::zh 阿里山森林鐵路也很特別。它帶旅客穿越森林和山景，也展現出台灣歷史中重要的一部分。

Visitors can also walk on forest trails, see giant old trees, and enjoy high mountain tea.
:::zh 旅客也可以走森林步道、欣賞巨大老樹，並品嚐高山茶。

I recommend Alishan to travelers who love nature, quiet scenery, and mountain culture.
:::zh 我推薦阿里山給喜歡自然、寧靜風景和山林文化的旅客。

## 30 秒短版

Alishan is one of Taiwan's most famous mountain destinations. It is known for sunrise, a sea of clouds, forest trails, old trees, and the forest railway. Visitors can also enjoy high mountain tea. I recommend Alishan to people who love nature and peaceful mountain scenery.
:::zh 阿里山是台灣最有名的山區目的地之一，以日出、雲海、森林步道、神木和森林鐵路聞名。旅客也可以品嚐高山茶。我推薦阿里山給喜歡自然和寧靜山景的人。

## 單字

- sunrise：日出
- sea of clouds：雲海
- forest trail：森林步道
- old tree：神木或老樹
- high mountain tea：高山茶
