# 台灣小吃 2 分鐘英文導覽稿：牛肉麵、小籠包、珍珠奶茶

## 可能抽到的題目

- 介紹台灣小吃
- 介紹最推薦外國旅客吃的台灣食物
- 介紹台灣飲食文化

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce three popular Taiwanese foods: beef noodles, xiaolongbao, and bubble tea.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹三種受歡迎的台灣食物：牛肉麵、小籠包和珍珠奶茶。

First, beef noodles are one of the most comforting dishes in Taiwan. A good bowl usually has tender beef, rich soup, and chewy noodles. It is simple, warm, and satisfying.
:::zh 首先，牛肉麵是台灣最有安慰感的料理之一。一碗好的牛肉麵通常有軟嫩牛肉、濃郁湯頭和有嚼勁的麵條。它簡單、溫暖又有飽足感。

Second, xiaolongbao are famous for their thin skin and hot soup inside. Visitors need to eat them carefully, because the soup can be very hot. This makes the experience fun and memorable.
:::zh 第二，小籠包以薄皮和裡面的熱湯汁聞名。旅客吃的時候要小心，因為湯汁很燙。這也讓體驗變得有趣又難忘。

Third, bubble tea is a creative Taiwanese drink. It combines tea, milk, and chewy tapioca pearls. Today, bubble tea is popular around the world, but trying it in Taiwan still feels special.
:::zh 第三，珍珠奶茶是一種有創意的台灣飲品。它結合茶、牛奶和有嚼勁的珍珠。現在珍珠奶茶風靡世界，但在台灣喝仍然很特別。

I think these foods show the friendly and creative side of Taiwan. They are easy to enjoy, and they help visitors understand local life through flavor.
:::zh 我認為這些食物展現出台灣友善又有創意的一面。它們容易入口，也能讓旅客透過味道理解在地生活。

## 30 秒短版

Taiwan has many famous foods. I would recommend beef noodles, xiaolongbao, and bubble tea. Beef noodles are warm and satisfying. Xiaolongbao are fun to eat because they have hot soup inside. Bubble tea is a creative drink from Taiwan. These foods are easy for visitors to enjoy and show the flavor of local life.
:::zh 台灣有很多有名的食物。我會推薦牛肉麵、小籠包和珍珠奶茶。牛肉麵溫暖又有飽足感。小籠包因為裡面有熱湯汁，吃起來很有趣。珍珠奶茶是來自台灣的創意飲品。這些食物容易入口，也展現在地生活的味道。

## 單字

- tender beef：軟嫩牛肉
- rich soup：濃郁湯頭
- chewy noodles：有嚼勁的麵條
- thin skin：薄皮
- tapioca pearls：珍珠
