# 台灣節慶 2 分鐘英文導覽稿：元宵燈會與端午龍舟

## 可能抽到的題目

- 介紹台灣節慶
- 介紹元宵節
- 介紹端午節

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce two important festivals in Taiwan: the Lantern Festival and the Dragon Boat Festival.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台灣兩個重要節慶：元宵節和端午節。

The Lantern Festival usually comes after Lunar New Year. During this festival, people enjoy colorful lantern displays, guess lantern riddles, and join local celebrations.
:::zh 元宵節通常在農曆新年之後。這個節日裡，人們會欣賞繽紛燈飾、猜燈謎，也會參加地方慶典。

For visitors, the Lantern Festival is a great chance to see Taiwan's creativity. Different cities often design lanterns with local culture, animals, technology, or traditional stories.
:::zh 對旅客來說，元宵節是看見台灣創意的好機會。不同城市常用地方文化、動物、科技或傳統故事設計燈飾。

The Dragon Boat Festival is also very famous. People watch dragon boat races and eat zongzi, a rice dumpling wrapped in bamboo leaves.
:::zh 端午節也很有名。人們會看龍舟比賽，並吃粽子，也就是用竹葉包起來的米食。

I like this festival because it combines sports, food, history, and teamwork. The races are exciting, and the food is full of family memories.
:::zh 我喜歡這個節慶，因為它結合運動、食物、歷史和團隊合作。比賽很刺激，食物也充滿家庭記憶。

These festivals show that Taiwanese culture is colorful, active, and closely connected to local communities.
:::zh 這些節慶展現出台灣文化色彩豐富、充滿活力，也和地方社群密切相關。

## 30 秒短版

Two important festivals in Taiwan are the Lantern Festival and the Dragon Boat Festival. During the Lantern Festival, people enjoy colorful lanterns and local events. During the Dragon Boat Festival, people watch dragon boat races and eat zongzi. These festivals show Taiwan's creativity, tradition, food culture, and community spirit.
:::zh 台灣兩個重要節慶是元宵節和端午節。元宵節時，人們會欣賞繽紛燈飾和地方活動。端午節時，人們會看龍舟比賽、吃粽子。這些節慶展現出台灣的創意、傳統、飲食文化和社群精神。

## 單字

- Lantern Festival：元宵節
- lantern display：燈飾展示
- dragon boat race：龍舟比賽
- bamboo leaves：竹葉
- community spirit：社群精神
