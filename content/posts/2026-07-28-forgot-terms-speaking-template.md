# 忘記專有名詞怎麼講：中文名稱加白話英文解釋

## 使用時機

忘記某個食物、習俗、景點的正式英文時，不要卡住。

可以直接講中文名稱，再補一句簡單英文。

## 萬用公式

中文名稱 + a kind of + 簡單分類 + 補味道或用途

## 英文模板

It is called Mi Tai Mu in Chinese. It is a kind of thick rice noodle dish, and it is popular in Taitung.
:::zh 它中文叫米苔目。它是一種粗米麵料理，在台東很受歡迎。

It is called zongzi in Chinese. It is a rice dumpling wrapped in bamboo leaves, and people usually eat it during the Dragon Boat Festival.
:::zh 它中文叫粽子。它是用竹葉包起來的米食，人們通常在端午節吃。

It is called betel nut in English, but if I explain it simply, it is a local chewing product in Taiwan.
:::zh 它英文叫 betel nut；簡單來說，它是台灣一種在地咀嚼物。

## 可替換句型

- I may not know the perfect English name, but I can explain it simply.
- It is a local food from Taiwan.
- It tastes sweet, salty, crispy, or chewy.
- It is often eaten during festivals.
- It is common in traditional markets or night markets.
