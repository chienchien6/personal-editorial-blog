# 台灣小吃英文口說總覽：從夜市到在地早餐怎麼介紹

台灣小吃是外語導遊口試非常好發揮的題目。

這類題目不需要背很多艱深菜名。重點是用簡單英文說清楚三件事：

- 這個食物是什麼
- 為什麼它有台灣特色
- 旅客可以在哪裡吃到

## 可能抽到的題目

- 介紹台灣小吃
- 介紹台灣夜市美食
- 介紹你最推薦外國旅客吃的台灣食物
- 介紹台灣飲食文化
- 規劃一個美食旅遊行程

## 2 分鐘英文導覽稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Taiwanese street food, one of the best ways to experience local life in Taiwan.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台灣小吃，這是體驗台灣在地生活最好的方式之一。

Taiwanese street food is popular because it is convenient, affordable, and full of local flavor. Visitors can find it in night markets, morning markets, old streets, and small family-run restaurants.
:::zh 台灣小吃受歡迎，是因為它方便、價格親民，而且充滿在地風味。旅客可以在夜市、早市、老街和家庭經營的小店裡找到它。

For example, beef noodles are warm and satisfying. Xiaolongbao are famous for their thin skin and hot soup inside. Bubble tea is a creative drink that has become popular around the world.
:::zh 例如，牛肉麵溫暖又有飽足感。小籠包以薄皮和裡面的熱湯汁聞名。珍珠奶茶則是一種有創意的飲品，現在已經風靡世界。

If visitors go to a night market, I would recommend trying oyster omelets, stinky tofu, scallion pancakes, and shaved ice. These foods show the lively and friendly side of Taiwanese culture.
:::zh 如果旅客去夜市，我會推薦蚵仔煎、臭豆腐、蔥油餅和剉冰。這些食物展現出台灣文化熱鬧又親切的一面。

In my opinion, Taiwanese street food is not only about eating. It is also about walking, talking, sharing, and enjoying the atmosphere with local people.
:::zh 我認為台灣小吃不只是吃東西，也包含散步、聊天、分享食物，以及和在地人一起享受氣氛。

That is why I always recommend foreign visitors to start their Taiwan trip with street food. Thank you.
:::zh 這也是為什麼我總是推薦外國旅客從小吃開始認識台灣。謝謝。

## 30 秒短版

Taiwanese street food is a great way to experience local culture. It is convenient, affordable, and full of flavor. Visitors can try beef noodles, xiaolongbao, bubble tea, oyster omelets, stinky tofu, and shaved ice. These foods are easy to find in night markets and old streets. I think street food shows the friendly and lively side of Taiwan.
:::zh 台灣小吃是體驗在地文化的好方法。它方便、價格親民，而且味道豐富。旅客可以品嚐牛肉麵、小籠包、珍珠奶茶、蚵仔煎、臭豆腐和剉冰。這些食物很容易在夜市和老街找到。我認為小吃展現出台灣友善又熱鬧的一面。

## 可替換句型

- It is crispy on the outside and soft inside.
- It is a popular snack at night markets.
- It may smell strong at first, but many people love the taste.
- It is a must-try food for first-time visitors.
- It shows the everyday flavor of Taiwan.
