# 日月潭英文導覽稿：湖景、原住民文化、自行車與遊船

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Sun Moon Lake, one of the most beautiful lakes in Taiwan.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹日月潭，台灣最美的湖泊之一。

Sun Moon Lake is located in central Taiwan. It is famous for its peaceful lake views, mountain scenery, cycling routes, temples, and Indigenous culture.
:::zh 日月潭位在台灣中部，以寧靜湖景、山景、自行車路線、廟宇和原住民文化聞名。

Visitors can take a boat on the lake and enjoy the scenery from different angles. They can also ride a bike around the lake, which is a relaxing way to experience the area.
:::zh 旅客可以搭船遊湖，從不同角度欣賞風景。也可以環湖騎自行車，用放鬆的方式體驗這個地區。

The area is also connected to the Thao Indigenous people. Visitors can learn about local culture and try local foods.
:::zh 這個地區也和邵族原住民有關。旅客可以認識在地文化，並品嚐地方食物。

I recommend Sun Moon Lake to visitors who want a relaxing trip with nature, culture, and outdoor activities.
:::zh 我推薦日月潭給想要結合自然、文化和戶外活動的放鬆行程的旅客。

## 30 秒短版

Sun Moon Lake is one of the most beautiful lakes in Taiwan. Visitors can take a boat, ride a bike around the lake, visit temples, and learn about Thao Indigenous culture. It is a relaxing destination with lake views, mountain scenery, and local food.
:::zh 日月潭是台灣最美的湖泊之一。旅客可以搭船、環湖騎自行車、參觀廟宇，並認識邵族文化。這是一個結合湖景、山景和在地美食的放鬆目的地。

## 單字

- lake view：湖景
- boat ride：搭船遊湖
- cycling route：自行車路線
- Indigenous culture：原住民文化
- relaxing destination：放鬆的目的地
