## 口說重點

安通溫泉可以用五個重點記：

- 地點：花東縱谷南段，靠近玉里與富里交界
- 特色：安靜、不商業化，有稻田、溪谷和山景
- 歷史：日治時期已被列為花蓮八景之一
- 泉質：弱鹼性氯化物硫酸鹽泉
- 行程：泡湯、自行車、輕健行、玉里小吃

## 開場：介紹安通溫泉

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Antong Hot Spring, a peaceful hot spring area in the southern part of the East Longitudinal Valley.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹安通溫泉，它是位在花東縱谷南段的一個寧靜溫泉區。

Unlike some busy tourist spots, Antong does not have a noisy shopping street. Instead, visitors can enjoy rice fields, mountain views, stream valleys, and a slower pace of travel.
:::zh 安通不像一些熱鬧的觀光景點，沒有吵雜的商店街。旅客可以在這裡欣賞稻田、山景、溪谷，並感受比較慢的旅行節奏。

## 自然風景：花東縱谷與玉里自行車道

Antong is located in the East Longitudinal Valley, which is known for its wide open views and peaceful countryside scenery.
:::zh 安通位在花東縱谷，這裡以開闊視野和寧靜的鄉村景色聞名。

In spring, the weather is mild, so it is a good season for outdoor activities. Visitors can ride bikes along the Yuli Bikeway and enjoy rice fields, forests, and the Central Mountain Range in the distance.
:::zh 春天氣候溫和，很適合戶外活動。旅客可以沿著玉里自行車道騎車，欣賞稻田、樹林，以及遠方的中央山脈。

This kind of trip is not about visiting as many attractions as possible. It is about slowing down and feeling the rhythm of local life.
:::zh 這樣的旅行不是為了踩很多景點，而是放慢速度，感受在地生活的節奏。

## 歷史：百年溫泉與日式建築

Antong Hot Spring is located near the Antong River, close to the boundary between Yuli Township and Fuli Township.
:::zh 安通溫泉位在安通溪附近，靠近玉里鎮與富里鄉的交界。

The hot spring area has more than one hundred years of history. During the Japanese colonial period, it was even listed as one of the Eight Views of Hualien.
:::zh 這個溫泉區有超過一百年的歷史。在日治時期，它甚至曾被列為花蓮八景之一。

Today, visitors can still see wooden single-story houses and tatami-style spaces, which quietly show the historical atmosphere of the area.
:::zh 今天，旅客仍然可以看到木造平房和榻榻米風格的空間，這些都靜靜保留著當地的歷史氛圍。

## 泉質與體驗

Antong Hot Spring is a weakly alkaline chloride-sulfate spring. For travelers, the most important thing is that it offers a relaxing and gentle hot spring experience.
:::zh 安通溫泉是弱鹼性氯化物硫酸鹽泉。對旅客來說，最重要的是它能提供放鬆、溫和的泡湯體驗。

After cycling or hiking, soaking in a hot spring is a perfect way to rest your body and enjoy the peaceful valley atmosphere.
:::zh 騎車或健行之後，泡溫泉是放鬆身體、享受縱谷寧靜氣氛的完美方式。

## 周邊行程與美食

A trip to Antong can also include light hiking on the Antong Traversing Trail. This trail combines nature and history, and it is suitable for travelers who want an easy outdoor experience.
:::zh 安通行程也可以安排安通越嶺道輕健行。這條路線結合自然與歷史，很適合想要輕鬆戶外體驗的旅客。

Nearby attractions may include Chike Mountain, Kecheng Iron Bridge, and Yuli Shinto Shrine. These places help visitors connect natural scenery with local culture.
:::zh 周邊景點可以包含赤科山、客城鐵橋和玉里神社。這些地方能讓旅客把自然景觀和地方文化連在一起。

For food, Yuli noodles, stinky tofu, and local ice desserts are popular choices. They are simple, local, and perfect after a day of walking or cycling.
:::zh 美食方面，玉里麵、臭豆腐和在地冰品都是受歡迎的選擇。這些食物簡單、有地方特色，也很適合走路或騎車一整天後享用。

## 結尾：推薦方式

I would recommend Antong to travelers who want a quiet and healing trip in eastern Taiwan.
:::zh 我會推薦安通給想在台灣東部安排安靜、療癒旅程的旅客。

You can plan a one-day trip with cycling and hot spring bathing, or stay for two days and one night to enjoy hiking, local food, and a slower rhythm of life.
:::zh 你可以安排一日遊，結合自行車和泡湯；也可以住兩天一夜，慢慢體驗健行、在地美食和比較慢的生活節奏。

In Antong, travel does not have to be fast. Sometimes, the best journey is the one that teaches us to slow down. Thank you.
:::zh 在安通，旅行不需要很快。有時候，最好的旅程，就是讓我們學會放慢速度的旅程。謝謝。

## 30 秒短版

Antong Hot Spring is a peaceful hot spring area in the East Longitudinal Valley. It is close to Yuli and Fuli, and it is surrounded by rice fields, mountains, and stream valleys. The area has more than one hundred years of history and was once listed as one of the Eight Views of Hualien. Visitors can ride bikes along the Yuli Bikeway, take a light hike, enjoy Yuli noodles and local snacks, and relax in a hot spring. I recommend Antong to travelers who want to slow down and experience the quiet beauty of eastern Taiwan.
:::zh 安通溫泉是花東縱谷裡一個寧靜的溫泉區，靠近玉里和富里，周圍有稻田、山景和溪谷。這個地區有超過一百年的歷史，曾經被列為花蓮八景之一。旅客可以沿著玉里自行車道騎車、安排輕健行、品嚐玉里麵和在地小吃，最後泡溫泉放鬆。我推薦安通給想放慢腳步、體驗台灣東部安靜之美的旅客。

## 可替換句型

如果考題是「介紹台灣溫泉」，可以說：

Taiwan has many hot spring areas, and Antong is a good example of a quiet and natural hot spring destination.

如果考題是「介紹東部旅遊」，可以說：

Eastern Taiwan is suitable for slow travel because visitors can enjoy mountains, rice fields, cycling routes, and local food.

如果考題是「介紹歷史景點」，可以說：

Antong Hot Spring has a long history from the Japanese colonial period, so it is not only a natural attraction but also a cultural site.
