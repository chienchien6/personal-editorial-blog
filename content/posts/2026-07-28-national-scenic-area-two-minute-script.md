# 台灣國家風景區 2 分鐘英文導覽稿：東北角、阿里山、日月潭

## 可能抽到的題目

- 介紹台灣國家風景區
- 介紹台灣自然景觀
- 規劃自然景點行程

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Taiwan's national scenic areas through three examples: the Northeast Coast, Alishan, and Sun Moon Lake.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想用三個例子介紹台灣國家風景區：東北角、阿里山和日月潭。

The Northeast Coast is famous for ocean views, rock formations, fishing villages, and coastal trails. It is a good choice for visitors who enjoy the sea and outdoor walks.
:::zh 東北角以海景、岩石地形、漁村和海岸步道聞名。它很適合喜歡海洋和戶外散步的旅客。

Alishan is one of the most famous mountain destinations in Taiwan. Visitors can watch the sunrise, walk through forest trails, see old trees, and take the historic forest railway.
:::zh 阿里山是台灣最有名的山區目的地之一。旅客可以看日出、走森林步道、欣賞神木，也可以搭乘歷史悠久的森林鐵路。

Sun Moon Lake is known for its beautiful lake view and relaxing atmosphere. Visitors can take a boat, ride a bike, visit temples, and learn about local Indigenous culture.
:::zh 日月潭以美麗湖景和放鬆氣氛聞名。旅客可以搭船、騎自行車、參觀廟宇，也可以認識當地原住民文化。

These places show how diverse Taiwan is. In a short trip, visitors can enjoy the ocean, mountains, lakes, culture, and local food.
:::zh 這些地方展現出台灣的多元。在短短一趟旅行中，旅客可以體驗海洋、山景、湖泊、文化和在地美食。

## 30 秒短版

Taiwan has many beautiful national scenic areas. The Northeast Coast is famous for ocean views and rock formations. Alishan is known for sunrise, forests, and the forest railway. Sun Moon Lake is popular for lake views, cycling, and Indigenous culture. These places show the natural beauty and diversity of Taiwan.
:::zh 台灣有許多美麗的國家風景區。東北角以海景和岩石地形聞名。阿里山以日出、森林和森林鐵路聞名。日月潭則以湖景、自行車和原住民文化受歡迎。這些地方展現出台灣的自然之美和多元性。

## 單字

- rock formation：岩石地形
- fishing village：漁村
- coastal trail：海岸步道
- forest railway：森林鐵路
- Indigenous culture：原住民文化
