# 台北英文導覽稿：城市交通、夜市、故宮與 101

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Taipei, the capital city of Taiwan and one of the best places for first-time visitors.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台北，台灣的首都，也是第一次來台灣的旅客很適合造訪的城市之一。

Taipei is convenient because it has a clean and efficient MRT system. Visitors can easily travel between famous attractions, night markets, museums, and shopping areas.
:::zh 台北很方便，因為它有乾淨又有效率的捷運系統。旅客可以輕鬆往返知名景點、夜市、博物館和購物區。

For landmarks, Taipei 101 is the most famous one. Visitors can enjoy the city view from the observatory and learn how this building became a symbol of modern Taiwan.
:::zh 如果是地標，台北 101 是最有名的一個。旅客可以從觀景台欣賞城市景色，也能了解這棟建築如何成為現代台灣的象徵。

For culture, I recommend the National Palace Museum. It has many important Chinese artworks and historical objects, so it is a good place to understand history and art.
:::zh 文化方面，我推薦故宮博物院。它收藏許多重要的中華藝術品和歷史文物，是理解歷史和藝術的好地方。

At night, visitors can go to night markets such as Shilin or Raohe. They can try street food, play games, and experience Taipei's lively local life.
:::zh 晚上，旅客可以去士林或饒河夜市。他們可以品嚐小吃、玩遊戲，也能體驗台北熱鬧的在地生活。

I think Taipei is a great starting point because it combines modern life, traditional culture, convenient transportation, and delicious food.
:::zh 我認為台北是很好的旅遊起點，因為它結合現代生活、傳統文化、便利交通和美味食物。

## 30 秒短版

Taipei is the capital city of Taiwan and a good place for first-time visitors. It has a convenient MRT system, famous landmarks such as Taipei 101, cultural places such as the National Palace Museum, and lively night markets. Visitors can experience modern Taiwan, traditional culture, and local food in one city.
:::zh 台北是台灣首都，也很適合第一次來台灣的旅客。它有便利捷運、台北 101 等知名地標、故宮等文化景點，以及熱鬧夜市。旅客可以在同一座城市體驗現代台灣、傳統文化和在地美食。

## 單字

- capital city：首都
- MRT system：捷運系統
- observatory：觀景台
- landmark：地標
- local life：在地生活
