# 台東英文導覽稿：熱氣球、月光海、縱谷與慢旅行

## 2 分鐘英文稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Taitung, one of the most relaxing destinations in eastern Taiwan.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台東，台灣東部最放鬆的目的地之一。

Taitung is known for open landscapes, ocean views, rice fields, Indigenous culture, and a slower pace of life.
:::zh 台東以開闊景觀、海景、稻田、原住民文化和較慢的生活節奏聞名。

In summer, the Taiwan International Balloon Festival in Luye Highland is a major highlight. Visitors can see colorful hot air balloons rise into the sky.
:::zh 夏天時，鹿野高台的台灣國際熱氣球嘉年華是一大亮點。旅客可以看到繽紛熱氣球升上天空。

Another special experience is the Moonlight Sea Concert on the East Coast. Visitors can listen to live music while watching the moon rise over the Pacific Ocean.
:::zh 另一個特別體驗是東海岸的月光海音樂會。旅客可以一邊聽現場音樂，一邊看月亮從太平洋升起。

Taitung is also suitable for cycling in Chishang, enjoying rice field views, trying local snacks, and visiting hot spring areas.
:::zh 台東也很適合在池上騎自行車、欣賞稻田、品嚐在地小吃，以及造訪溫泉區。

I recommend Taitung to travelers who want to slow down, enjoy nature, and experience the peaceful side of Taiwan.
:::zh 我推薦台東給想放慢腳步、享受自然，並體驗台灣寧靜一面的旅客。

## 30 秒短版

Taitung is a relaxing destination in eastern Taiwan. It is famous for ocean views, rice fields, Indigenous culture, and slow travel. Visitors can enjoy the Hot Air Balloon Festival in Luye, the Moonlight Sea Concert on the East Coast, and cycling in Chishang. Taitung is perfect for travelers who love nature and peaceful scenery.
:::zh 台東是台灣東部很放鬆的目的地，以海景、稻田、原住民文化和慢旅行聞名。旅客可以欣賞鹿野熱氣球嘉年華、東海岸月光海音樂會，也可以在池上騎自行車。台東很適合喜歡自然和寧靜風景的旅客。
