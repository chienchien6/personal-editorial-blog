# 台灣文化習俗英文口說總覽：廟宇、紅包、拜拜與日常禮俗

文化習俗題最容易講得太抽象。

準備時不要只說 Taiwan has rich culture。要直接舉例：

- 廟宇與拜拜
- 農曆新年和紅包
- 中秋團圓
- 夜市和廟口生活
- 台灣人的熱情與禮貌

- 介紹台灣文化習俗

## 2 分鐘英文導覽稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce Taiwanese cultural customs. Taiwan's culture is a mix of tradition, religion, family values, and everyday local life.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台灣文化習俗。台灣文化結合了傳統、宗教、家庭價值和日常在地生活。

One important part of Taiwanese culture is temple culture. Many people visit temples to pray for health, safety, good luck, or success. For visitors, temples are also great places to see traditional architecture, local festivals, and community life.
:::zh 台灣文化中很重要的一部分是廟宇文化。許多人會去廟裡祈求健康、平安、好運或成功。對旅客來說，廟宇也是欣賞傳統建築、地方節慶和社群生活的好地方。

Another important custom is giving red envelopes during Lunar New Year. Older family members give red envelopes to children or younger relatives as a blessing for good luck and a new beginning.
:::zh 另一個重要習俗是農曆新年發紅包。長輩會給小孩或晚輩紅包，象徵祝福、好運和新的開始。

Food is also closely connected to culture. During festivals, families gather and eat special foods, such as rice dumplings during the Dragon Boat Festival or mooncakes during Mid-Autumn Festival.
:::zh 食物也和文化密切相關。節慶時，家人會聚在一起吃特別的食物，例如端午節吃粽子，中秋節吃月餅。

In daily life, Taiwanese people are usually friendly and helpful. If visitors get lost, many locals are willing to give directions or recommend good food nearby.
:::zh 在日常生活中，台灣人通常友善又樂於助人。如果旅客迷路，很多在地人都願意指路，或推薦附近好吃的食物。

I think Taiwanese customs are special because they are not only seen in museums. They are part of everyday life, in temples, markets, families, and neighborhoods.
:::zh 我認為台灣習俗很特別，因為它們不只存在博物館裡，而是存在日常生活中，例如廟宇、市場、家庭和街坊鄰里。

## 30 秒短版

Taiwanese cultural customs are closely connected to religion, family, food, and daily life. Many people visit temples to pray for health and good luck. During Lunar New Year, older family members give red envelopes as blessings. During festivals, families gather and eat special foods. I think these customs show the warm and friendly side of Taiwanese culture.
:::zh 台灣文化習俗和宗教、家庭、食物與日常生活密切相關。許多人會到廟裡祈求健康和好運。農曆新年時，長輩會發紅包表示祝福。節慶時，家人會聚在一起吃特別食物。我認為這些習俗展現出台灣文化溫暖又友善的一面。

## 可替換句型

- This custom is related to family and good wishes.
- Visitors can experience this tradition in temples or local communities.
- Food plays an important role in this festival.
- This custom shows how Taiwanese people value family.
- It is a good example of everyday culture in Taiwan.
