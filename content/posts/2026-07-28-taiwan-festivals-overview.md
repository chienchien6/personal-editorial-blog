# 台灣節慶英文口說總覽：元宵、端午、中秋與地方慶典

台灣節慶題很適合展現文化導覽感。

回答時不要只背日期。比較好的方式是講出：

- 節慶做什麼
- 它代表什麼文化意義
- 旅客可以怎麼參與

## 可能抽到的題目

- 介紹台灣節慶
- 介紹元宵節或燈會
- 介紹端午節
- 介紹中秋節
- 介紹一個地方特色活動

## 2 分鐘英文導覽稿

Good morning or afternoon. Today, I would like to introduce festivals in Taiwan. Taiwanese festivals are colorful, lively, and closely connected to family, religion, food, and local communities.
:::zh 各位考官早安或午安。今天我想介紹台灣節慶。台灣節慶色彩豐富、氣氛熱鬧，而且和家庭、宗教、食物與地方社群密切相關。

One of the most famous festivals is the Lantern Festival. During this festival, people enjoy beautiful lantern displays, guess lantern riddles, and visit local events. It is a great time for visitors to see the creative side of Taiwan.
:::zh 最有名的節慶之一是元宵節。人們會欣賞美麗燈飾、猜燈謎，也會參加地方活動。這是旅客看見台灣創意面貌的好時機。

Another important festival is the Dragon Boat Festival. People watch dragon boat races and eat rice dumplings called zongzi. This festival shows teamwork, tradition, and the importance of remembering history.
:::zh 另一個重要節慶是端午節。人們會看龍舟比賽，也會吃粽子。這個節慶展現團隊合作、傳統，以及記得歷史的重要性。

Mid-Autumn Festival is also very popular. Families and friends gather together, enjoy mooncakes, have barbecue, and look at the full moon. It is a warm festival about reunion.
:::zh 中秋節也很受歡迎。家人和朋友會聚在一起，吃月餅、烤肉、賞月。這是一個關於團圓的溫暖節日。

Besides these national festivals, Taiwan also has many local celebrations, such as the Taiwan Lantern Festival, the Yanshui Beehive Fireworks Festival, and the Taitung Hot Air Balloon Festival.
:::zh 除了這些全國性節慶，台灣也有很多地方活動，例如台灣燈會、鹽水蜂炮和台東熱氣球嘉年華。

I think festivals are one of the best ways for foreign visitors to understand Taiwan because they can experience culture through food, music, colors, and people.
:::zh 我認為節慶是外國旅客理解台灣最好的方式之一，因為他們可以透過食物、音樂、色彩和人群來體驗文化。

## 30 秒短版

Taiwanese festivals are colorful and lively. For example, during the Lantern Festival, people enjoy lantern displays and local events. During the Dragon Boat Festival, people watch dragon boat races and eat zongzi. During Mid-Autumn Festival, families gather to eat mooncakes and enjoy the full moon. I think festivals are a great way for visitors to experience Taiwanese culture.
:::zh 台灣節慶色彩豐富又熱鬧。例如元宵節時，人們會欣賞燈飾和參加地方活動。端午節時，人們會看龍舟比賽、吃粽子。中秋節時，家人會團聚吃月餅、賞月。我認為節慶是旅客體驗台灣文化的好方式。

## 可替換句型

- This festival is full of energy and local color.
- Visitors can join the event and experience local culture.
- It is not only a festival, but also a community gathering.
- Food is an important part of this celebration.
- This event is especially popular among families and tourists.

## 實用單字

- festival：節慶
- lantern display：燈飾展示
- lantern riddle：燈謎
- dragon boat race：龍舟比賽
- rice dumpling / zongzi：粽子
- mooncake：月餅
- reunion：團圓
- local celebration：地方慶典
- fireworks：煙火或蜂炮
- community gathering：社群聚會
